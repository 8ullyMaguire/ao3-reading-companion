Backup & data

Export your full extension state to a file, restore from a previous backup, or wipe everything and start over.

Export

Saves everything — preferences, history, fic cache, author affinity, dislike history, custom rows, and settings — as a single JSON file.

Import

Replaces every store with the contents of the backup. Current data is wiped.

Import AO3 bookmarks

Walks your AO3 bookmarks (newest first) and adds each bookmarked work to your Save for Later list. Skips anything already in your reading history — existing state is never overwritten. Politely spaced to respect AO3's servers; large bookmark lists can take several minutes.

Reset to defaults

Wipes preferences, reading history, fic cache, author affinity, dislike history, custom rows, and subscribed authors. Settings reset to defaults. You'll be sent back to onboarding.
