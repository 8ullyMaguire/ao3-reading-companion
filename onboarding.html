What does this extension actually do?

AO3 famously has no algorithm — works are sorted by date or filtered by your search, and that's it. This extension adds a personal recommendation algorithm just for you:

  • It quietly notices the fics you visit on AO3 and saves their metadata to a database in your browser.
  • You teach it your tastes (this setup is the first step) and it scores fics for you using a transparent formula you can see at any time.
  • It builds you a feed with rows like "Similar to your tastes," "Hidden gems," "Your authors," and any custom rows you define.
  • Every recommendation has a "why this?" button that explains exactly why it's there.

There's no server, no account, no telemetry. The extension only talks to AO3 itself, only as much as needed.
